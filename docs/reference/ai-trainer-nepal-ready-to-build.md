# 3. Overall UI/UX Direction

Google + Apple inspired, humanized. Clean, premium, calm, spacious, modern,
image-heavy, trustworthy, personal, editorial rather than corporate, technical
without feeling overly technical.

Avoid a typical "AI website" appearance: glowing gradients, floating robots,
excessive glassmorphism, neon colors, generic stock imagery.

Communicate: "A real person who understands technology and teaches it to real people."

**Apple-inspired:** large typography, strong whitespace, minimal navigation,
large visual moments, clear hierarchy, smooth section transitions, product-like
presentation, very few unnecessary UI elements.

**Google-inspired:** accessible layouts, clear information architecture,
answer-first content, helpful cards, search-friendly organization, familiar
interaction patterns, human-centered communication.

**Humanized layer (critical):** real photographs of Arjun teaching, speaking at
events, training rooms, students participating, corporate sessions, workshops,
laptop/workspace moments, screens showing AI workflows, audience interaction,
behind-the-scenes preparation. Real photography carries more trust than generic
AI-generated illustrations.

# 25. UI COMPONENT RECOMMENDATIONS

**Sticky navigation:** minimal — About | AI Training | AI Consultation |
Research | Blog. CTA: Book Training. Compact sticky CTA on mobile.

**Buttons:** one primary style. Primary: Book AI Training. Secondary: Explore
Training. Avoid 5 different CTA styles.

**Cards:** use sparingly. Prefer editorial sections and large visual blocks over
a page made entirely from cards.

**Typography:** modern system font stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF
Pro Display", "SF Pro Text", "Inter", "Segoe UI", sans-serif). Large responsive
headings. Hero H1 approx: desktop 64-80px, tablet 48-60px, mobile 40-48px. Keep
line lengths short.

**Border radius:** restrained, approx 16-24px for large image/cards. Avoid every
element becoming a pill.

**Animation:** subtle fade-in, image reveal, small translate, smooth hover,
scroll-based section transitions. Avoid constant floating elements, excessive
parallax, fast animations, decorative motion everywhere. Respect
`prefers-reduced-motion`.
