# AI Training & AI Courses Website Content Strategy
## Arjun Basnet | arjun-basnet.com.np

**Document purpose:** Master content, SEO, information architecture, trust, internal-linking, and implementation guide for the AI Training and AI Courses sections of the website.

**Primary market:** Nepal, with scope for international learners and organizations where appropriate.

**Core website:** https://arjun-basnet.com.np/

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This guide defines a scalable content architecture for positioning Arjun Basnet's website around practical AI training, AI courses, AI workshops, AI consultation, and AI automation.

The strategy is designed to:

- Build topical authority around AI training and AI education.
- Capture industry-specific search intent without creating thin keyword pages.
- Separate organization-focused AI training from individual AI courses.
- Build trust through verifiable credentials, projects, research, testimonials, and case studies.
- Create strong internal links between service pages, industry pages, course pages, and supporting articles.
- Develop content that is useful to people first and search engines second.
- Create a structure that can be implemented cleanly in Next.js.
- Establish a repeatable framework for future courses, industries, workshops, and resources.

The website should not become a collection of pages that simply replace one keyword with another. Every page must have a clear search intent, distinct audience, unique information, relevant evidence, and a useful next step.

The website should position Arjun Basnet as a practical AI educator, trainer, consultant, and technology professional.

# 53. SEO Anti-Patterns to Avoid

Do not:

- Create dozens of near-identical industry pages.
- Change only the keyword and city name.
- Invent statistics.
- Invent testimonials.
- Invent case studies.
- Use fake client logos.
- Claim certifications that do not exist.
- Claim guaranteed business results.
- Overuse keywords.
- Stuff keywords into headings unnaturally.
- Create course pages for courses that do not actually exist.
- Publish "certificate" claims without a real certificate process.
- Make medical or financial claims without appropriate evidence.
- Treat AI as a replacement for qualified professionals.
- Create pages solely because a keyword has search volume.

The objective is a useful website, not a keyword directory.

# 58. Final Principle

The goal is not to create the largest number of AI pages.

The goal is to create the **most useful and trustworthy AI learning and training resource around the services you can actually deliver**.

Each page should answer five questions:

1. **Who is this for?**
2. **What problem does it solve?**
3. **What will the person or organization learn or accomplish?**
4. **Why should they trust the instructor or service?**
5. **What should they do next?**

If a page cannot answer those questions with real information, it should not be published yet.

The strongest long-term structure is therefore:

**AI Training + AI Courses + AI Workshops + AI Consultation + AI Automation + authoritative supporting content + verifiable evidence + strong internal linking.**

This creates a coherent AI-focused section of arjun-basnet.com.np rather than a collection of disconnected SEO landing pages.
