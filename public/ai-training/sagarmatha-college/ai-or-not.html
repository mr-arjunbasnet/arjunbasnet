How to check, now that looking isn't enough

  • Read the text in the image. Shop signs, newspapers, number plates. AI often garbles lettering, especially Devanagari, though newer AI gets it right.
  • Count the small things. Fingers, fork prongs, spokes, railings. AI gets the overall shape right and the counts wrong.
  • Look for "too perfect". Identical momos, golden light everywhere, a crowd with no one blinking or mid-step.
  • Ask where it came from. Who posted it first? Is there an original with a date and a name? A reverse image search takes ten seconds.
  • Don't rely on looking. The best fakes have no visible tell. Verify the source, not the pixels.
