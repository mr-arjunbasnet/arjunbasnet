Brand story
ARJUN represents focused learning, purposeful action and the journey from challenge to possibility. The brand brings Arjun’s focus and discipline into modern technology and AI, helping people turn problems into practical solutions.
FocusA single point of intent: see only what matters, then act.
JourneyRising forms and mountain paths: growth through challenge.

TransformationProblem, understanding, solution, impact.

Build order for every piece of work: person, purpose, problem, technology, solution, impact. Arjun’s personal brand comes first; AI is the tool, not the headline.

The mark

Four ideas, one simple form

The mark is deliberately abstract. It should be recognised in a second and understood slowly: the meaning is discovered, never illustrated.

A for Arjun

The two rising strokes form the initial of the name. Personal identity comes first; everything else is built on it.

The arrow of progress

The rising strokes carry forward, up-and-right momentum: movement from uncertainty toward clarity, from problem toward solution.

The peak

Read as a whole, the A becomes a Himalayan summit: growth, challenge and the long climb of learning.

The focus spark

A guiding point of light above the summit: focus on what matters, and the guidance that shows the right direction when a problem is complex.

Logo system

A clear hierarchy for every space

Use the most complete version the space allows. Step down only when the slogan would fall below its minimum size.

Construction

Built on one angle

Every principal stroke leans at roughly 59°, so the A, the ribbon and the rise all move in one direction. The A is a single continuous shape with one gradient. Keep the geometry intact: never redraw, re-space or re-proportion the mark.

Clear space: x

x equals the height of the spark. Keep at least x of empty space on every side of the mark or any lockup, measured at the size used.

Shared angle

The A’s outer strokes and the ribbon all lean at 58–60° (dashed guides). The gap between the A and the ribbon stays constant from base to top.

One shape, one gradient

The A is drawn as a single outline, so its gradient flows without seams. The spark is one solid form and stays intact at every size, down to the 16 px favicon.

Lockup proportions

ARJUN is set in Montserrat SemiBold with 0.34 em tracking. The slogan in Montserrat Medium is justified to the exact width of the wordmark.

Colourways

Gradient first, one colour when needed

Full colour on light backgrounds is the default. Reversed and one-colour versions keep the mark working on dark surfaces, photography and in production methods that cannot print gradients.

Colour

Variant Aurora

A controlled move from cool blue through violet into a touch of magenta. The gradient is a brand asset, not decoration: use it on the mark, in hero moments and on key actions, supported by generous Warm White and Deep Indigo.

* Print note: Aurora Blue, Violet and Magenta, plus Iris, sit outside the CMYK gamut. The CMYK values shown are the closest match on coated stock (FOGRA39), and will look noticeably duller than on screen. For vivid business cards and merchandise, use extended-gamut digital print or spot inks matched from a physical Pantone guide, and always approve a printed proof.
